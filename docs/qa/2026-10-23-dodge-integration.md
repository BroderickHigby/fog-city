# Dodge and world-surface integration — 2026-10-23 UTC

The continuation branch merges `feature/dodge` into the Ferry showcase plus the reviewed
`qa/sim` world surfaces. Neither v4 implementation replaced the other. Cars and agents retain
the deck/tunnel floors, rails, stair barriers and terrain handling, and the dodge retains its
vehicle-body clearance and safe joins/exits.

The combined contract is **SIM_VERSION 5**. Its migration loads saves from v1-v3 and both
historical v4 branches. Missing dodge fields are initialized; existing dives, near-miss counts,
other extension data and poses are preserved. The driving, crowded-sidewalk and deck/tunnel
replay hashes were generated by executing the merged runtime and committed in
`tests/sim/replay.test.ts`.

A new integration regression exposed that ambient dive planning and placement still used raw
terrain height. On a bridge that dropped an NPC through its deck; in a tunnel it lifted the NPC
into the hill. Both paths now use the agent's road surface. Dedicated tests cover this and a
car driving at a teammate on the actual deck and tunnel fixtures.

Validation (one foreground job at a time, 8 GB Node heap limit, at most two Vitest workers):

- Typecheck passed.
- Final focused sim run: **13 files, 163 tests passed**, including replay migration/pins,
  surface+dodge integration, surfaces, three dodge review/fuzz/soak lanes, walker offsets,
  on-foot behavior, ambient agents, vehicle pushes and the forbidden-API guard.
- Render/harness run: **3 files, 50 tests passed** (dodge poses, agent models, E2E harness).
- The initial broader sim run passed 298 tests and failed only the two new ambient-surface
  reproductions before their fix. The final focused run reran those against the corrected code.

This validates the merge, not the whole game's release readiness. Existing generated-city
and world-gate blockers remain documented in `2026-10-23-integration-status.md`; no baseline
or assertion was relaxed. Browser cross-engine checks were not run in this lane.
